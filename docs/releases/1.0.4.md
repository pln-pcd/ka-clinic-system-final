# K&A Clinic 1.0.4 — 10 October 2026

Android Back now returns Account subpages to Account. The Chat tab previously
kept a global Back listener alive while other tabs were visible, and Account
subpages did not consume the native Back event. Back handlers now subscribe
only while their screen is focused. The same fix covers Personal Information,
Privacy Policy, Logged-in Devices and the staff conversation view. Appointment
and Home popups handle Back locally, and password recovery has a sign-in fallback
when opened without history.

KAI shows the clinic logo and one persistent, shorter disclaimer:
“No medical advice. For urgent or medical concerns, contact the clinic or a
licensed healthcare professional.” The test-environment banner and duplicate
footer disclaimer are removed from both chatbot components. Study sessions
still use test prompts only. Navigation and chatbot instructions spell out
Appointments.

Vaccination card faces share their larger measured content height, so flipping
does not resize the card or move following sections. Card table values wrap.
The signup arrow follows the text baseline. Appointment notices wrap within
padded borders; guest sessions skip protected patient appointment and staff
thread queries. Dose timelines use responsive cards with complete session,
vaccine, dosage/route, date and status fields. Onboarding preloads illustrations
and uses a short native slide transition without the former 800 ms content fades.
