# K&A Clinic 1.0.13 — 23 October 2026

## Fixes & Improvements

- **Appointment System Rules & Dashboard Overhaul**:
  - **Confirm Only**: Clinic staff can only confirm appointments, never decline. Removed the Decline button, decline dialog/reason, and decline notifications across admin and patient apps.
  - **Direct Patient Cancellation**: Patients can now cancel Pending and Confirmed appointments directly from the appointment details modal. Tapping "Cancel Appointment" opens a confirmation prompt with an optional non-blocking reason (up to 300 characters). Cancellation takes effect immediately, releases the booked slot in real-time, and updates the status to "Cancelled by patient" without requiring admin approval.
  - **Patient Self-Service Rescheduling**: Added a "Reschedule" button in Appointment Details for pending and confirmed upcoming appointments. Implemented a 3-step interactive modal with weekend calendar selection, balanced slot picker (displaying "Current" for the active slot and disabling taken slots), and a review step with editable notes. Rescheduling executes via an atomic transaction that simultaneously locks the new slot and releases the previous slot. Confirmed appointments return to Pending status with real-time feedback.
  - **Notification Parity**: Standardized appointment confirmation notification title to `"Appointment confirmed by the clinic"` and body to `"Your appointment on <Date> <Time> was confirmed by the clinic."` with zero emojis.

- **KAI Virtual Assistant AI Grounding & Conversational Intelligence**:
  - **Grounded with Official App Guide**: Injected the full `appGuide.md` knowledge base into Gemini system instructions, enabling KAI to answer specific questions accurately (e.g. app identity, screen navigation, rescheduling instructions, office hours, and clinic location) rather than dumping generic 4-bullet menus.
  - **Multi-Turn Context & Multilingual Fluency**: Added full conversational history tracking so follow-up queries resolve naturally. KAI now fluently responds in the patient's language (English, Filipino / Tagalog, or Taglish).
  - **Strict Clinical Safety Boundaries**: Maintained immediate deterministic refusal for all medical queries, symptoms, bites, and medication/dose regimens. Refusal messages preserve the exact, verbatim disclaimer: *"This chatbot does not provide medical advice. For urgent or medical concerns, contact the clinic or a licensed healthcare professional."* and highlight the "Talk to clinic staff" button.
  - **Patient Privacy & Honest Errors**: Personal schedule and MRN inquiries direct the patient to their digital vaccination card on the Home screen. If the AI model or network is unreachable, KAI transparently reports failure without faking canned answers.

- **Clinic Dashboard & Nurse Management Updates**:
  - **Archive Service Controls**: Removed the disabled `+ Add Another Service` button on unrestored archived patient records. The active emerald `+ Add Another Service` button is now shown only for records that are active or restored and whose dose series is completed.
  - **Manage Staff Role Terminology**: Updated staff role badges and subtitles in Manage Staff to display `NURSE` and `Staff Nurse` instead of `STAFF` and `Team Member`.
  - **Animated Nurse Mascot on Registration**: Added an animated vector nurse character with CSS micro-animations (waving arm, blinking eyes, breathing bob, and floating sparkles) and a reactive speech bubble to the right panel of the nurse registration card (`/accept-invite`).
