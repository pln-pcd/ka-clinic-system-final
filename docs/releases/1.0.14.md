# K&A Clinic 1.0.14 — 24 October 2026

## Fixes & Improvements

- **Clinic Schedule Closures & Pre-Booking Transparency**:
  - **Schedule Closures**: Staff can close specific whole days or specific time slots with predefined reasons (Doctor unavailable, Holiday, Clinic event, System maintenance, or Other) and a custom message shown to patients (up to 150 characters).
  - **Private Staff Notes**: Added a protected internal note field for staff, securely isolated in an admin-only subcollection (`/private/meta`) inaccessible to patients or public clients.
  - **Live Concurrency & Visual Calendar**: Real-time Firestore sync locks closed weekend days and slots immediately. Closed days display with a lock icon, and partially closed days show a lock badge.
  - **Custom K&A Closure Modal**: Tapping a closed day or slot opens the custom branded modal with the clinic's message quote box, clinic walk-in hours reminder, "Pick another date", and a "Show next available" button that auto-navigates to the nearest open appointment slot.
  - **Atomic Transaction Enforcement**: Server-side and transactional checks block double-booking or booking on closed dates/slots with HTTP 409 code `SCHEDULE_CLOSED`.
  - **Affected Appointment Alerts**: Existing appointments inside closed periods are preserved; affected patients receive push notifications, formatted emails, and an "Affected by closure" banner with direct "Reschedule" and "Cancel appointment" actions.

- **Appointment Cancellation Policy Realignment**:
  - Clinic staff and administrators can no longer cancel patient appointments.
  - Appointments can only be cancelled by the patients themselves from the patient app.
  - Cryptographically enforced via Firestore Security Rules prohibiting admin updates with status `cancelled`.

- **KAI Virtual Assistant Grounding**:
  - Live active closures are ingested into KAI's live clinic knowledge base so questions regarding clinic open dates and hours are answered accurately and conversationally.
