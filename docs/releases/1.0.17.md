# K&A Clinic 1.0.17 — 27 October 2026

## Patient App

- **Schedule closure notices**: When the clinic closes a day or time slots, every app user now gets a pop-up (and a push notification if notifications are on). A notice that arrived while the app was closed pops up the next time the app is opened.
- **Affected appointments**: The "Affected by schedule closure" card on an appointment uses calmer, neutral colours.

## Clinic Dashboard & Server (live on the web, no app update needed)

- Closures are announced to every patient: app users in the app, patients without the app by email.
- Calendar no longer shows days from the previous or next month; the closure banner uses neutral colours.
- KAI answers how-to questions with complete, numbered steps.
