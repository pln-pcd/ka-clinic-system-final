# K&A Clinic 1.0.11 — 17 October 2026

## Fixes & Improvements

- **Past Treatment Vaccination Card Size & Tag**:
  - Removed previous treatment tag from historical vaccination cards so card dimensions and layout match the active home vaccination card 1:1.
  - Eliminated the redundant "PREVIOUS TREATMENT" badge and date range from the card.
- **Chatbot Vaccine Pricing & Services Routing**:
  - Fixed false-positive clinical block when asking combined questions like "What vaccines and services do you offer?".
  - Restored published pricing for vaccine services (PEP Category 2, PEP Category 3 complete package, and Pneumococcal vaccine) and Hepatitis discontinuation notice in KAI replies and system instructions.
- **Logged-in Devices Deduplication**:
  - Active sessions belonging to the same device (e.g. repeated logins or reinstalls on the same phone) are deduplicated into a single entry displaying the latest activity.
  - Revoking a device revokes all underlying sessions for that device.
  - Prunes orphaned duplicate session records for the same device on registration.
