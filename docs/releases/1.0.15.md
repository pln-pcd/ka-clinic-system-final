# K&A Clinic 1.0.15 — 27 October 2026

## Patient App

- **KAI Virtual Assistant**: Removed the outdated Hepatitis vaccine discontinuation notice from KAI replies.

## Clinic Dashboard & Server (live on the web, no app update needed)

- **Welcome email on registration**: New patients receive an email confirming they are registered, with their MRN, a link to download this app, and steps to sign up and sign in.
- **Walk-in booking safeguard**: A walk-in booked by staff can't overwrite an hour a patient already holds.
- **Email reminders**: The "I received this notification" link now asks for a tap to confirm, so email security scanners can no longer confirm reminders on a patient's behalf.
- **KAI chatbot**: Rate limited per device to protect the assistant's daily quota.
