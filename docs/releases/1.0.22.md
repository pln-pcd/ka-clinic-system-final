## Changes

- KAI uses one shared set of approved clinic facts and English, Filipino and Taglish replies in the patient app and API.
- Medical questions receive the fixed staff handoff and a matching disclaimer. Personal details are not repeated; personal record questions point to the vaccination card.
- Live availability respects occupied appointments, slot holds, elapsed times and clinic schedule closures. Unavailable data never becomes an invented open slot.
- The mobile app keeps its vaccination-card navigation buttons and highlighted staff-chat control. Staff chat opens when the patient chooses it.
- Removed the “HIPAA & DOH Compliant” text from the web login footer.
- Added a scoped, repeatable script for the five complete overdue sample patients. The displayed names have no Demo prefix; the records retain their sample markers and fictional-data notes.
