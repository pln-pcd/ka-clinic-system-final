# K&A Clinic 1.0.18 — 27 October 2026

## Patient App

- **Appointments in a clinic closure**: Can no longer be cancelled; the patient reschedules to another date or time slot instead. Rescheduling clears the "Affected by schedule closure" notice.
- **"Appointments closed" pop-up**: Calmer, neutral design.
- **Enlarged vaccination card**: The dose table fills the card, with roomier rows and no empty space below it.

## Security Rules

- A patient cannot cancel an appointment that falls inside a clinic closure, and can clear (never set) the closure flag when rescheduling. Older app versions can still reschedule.
