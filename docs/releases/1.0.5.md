# K&A Clinic 1.0.5 — 16 October 2026

## Patient app

**Vaccination card.** Front and back are one fixed size: both faces are
measured, the taller one sets the height, and the shorter face fills it with
its footer at the bottom. The exported (ViewShot) front and back images are
the same size. Every field comes from the current case on
`vaccinations/{mrn}` using the dashboard's service-to-fields rules: only PEP
shows exposure fields (date, type, biting animal, category, number of bites,
wound washed); PrEP and other services never show a PEP label or schedule.
Wound location and depth, and the clinic's internal remarks, are never shown.
The back now shows age, sex, contact, the service's medical history, the dose
schedule, doses given and case status. The header carries the K&A logo on the
left and the Libon municipal seal on the right (front, back, enlarged view and
exports). The clinic name grew from `moderateScale(9.5)` to
`moderateScale(10.5)` inline and from 10.5 to 12 in the enlarged view, and may
wrap between the two logos.

**Previous Services.** After the dose timeline, earlier services saved under
the same MRN (`caseHistory`) are listed: service, date started, doses given,
completion date, status, and exposure category for PEP only. Nothing renders
when there is no earlier service.

**Appointments.** The appointment type is a floating, scrollable dropdown
(tap outside or Back to close). "Follow-up" is removed: the rabies option is
"PEP". New bookings store `type: "PEP"`; older `"PEP Follow-up"` bookings
display as "PEP" in both apps. An optional "Patient notes" field (300
characters, with counter) is saved as `notes` and shown in Appointment Details
and in the dashboard. Request Cancellation is navy. My Appointments cards show
the type, date and time, with the status badge and a "Cancel" action on one
line. Screen content uses a 16 dp minimum side gutter.

**KAI.** The disclaimer container above the conversation is gone. The
disclaimer is one quiet line in the welcome screen and at the top of each
conversation, never under the input.

**Home.** The "Sign up to track your doses" arrow is centred on its text.

**Start-up and sign-in.** Returning patients skip the 2.6 s animated intro:
the app opens Home (signed in) or sign-in directly behind the native splash.
The sign-in server is woken while the patient types, the trust check reads
the keystore and ID token in parallel, and the server returns the session's
MRN with the second factor, so Home no longer waits for a separate MRN lookup.
The sign-in screen enters as one top-to-bottom sequence; each block fades as a
single layer so no button or shadow appears before its card.

## One MRN per patient

- `getAndIncrementNextMRN` runs in a transaction only (no local fallback),
  skips any number already registered or in use, and records every MRN in the
  permanent `mrnRegistry`. `settings/counters` can only move forward.
- Add Another Service no longer overwrites `authorizedPatients.uid` and
  `registered` (it used to write null/false from the vaccinations row). Live
  data check: no patient had been affected.
- Sign-in resolves exactly one MRN (by uid, or by email only for a record with
  no uid) and refuses an account linked to more than one; the typed MRN must
  belong to the signing-in account. Seed/reset scripts no longer rewind the
  counter or reuse issued MRNs.
- `scripts/audit-mrn.mjs` (read-only) audits live data. 16 October 2026: 65
  records, 0 shared MRNs, 0 emails or uids on more than one MRN, 0 missing
  mappings, counter at 2026-053.

## Admin dashboard

- "Manage Admins" is now "Manage Staff" (sidebar, heading, browser tab). The
  route id and stored roles are unchanged.
- Manage Staff → Activity Log (managers only): append-only `activityLog`
  entries for patient registration and edits, Add Another Service, dose and
  RIG/Anti-Tetanus changes, reports, follow-ups, appointment bookings,
  confirmations, declines, cancellations and cancellation decisions,
  stock changes, messages, profile changes, and staff invites, revokes and
  deletions (server-side). Filters: staff, action, date range, MRN; search on
  the loaded page; 25 per page; full before/after detail.
- Patient notes show on booking-request cards and in the appointment preview.

## Firestore rules

- Patients read `vaccinations/{mrn}` (including `caseHistory`) only for the
  MRN of their verified session.
- `mrnRegistry`: staff read/create, never update or delete.
  `settings/counters`: forward-only, never deleted.
- `activityLog`: managers read; staff create only as themselves, with their
  live role and server time; nobody updates or deletes.
- Patient bookings: `notes` optional, at most 300 characters.
