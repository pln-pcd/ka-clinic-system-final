# K&A Clinic 1.0.29 — 3 November 2026

## Highlights & Changes

### 1. Official Version 1.0.29 (Version Code: 29)
- Incremented `versionCode` to **29** (`1.0.29`) ensuring Android devices with previous releases can perform a clean in-place update without uninstallation.

### 2. Patient Intake & Optional Email Registration
- **Optional Email**: Patient registration wizard now treats email as optional with guidance that an email is required for patient companion app sign-in. Blank emails are cleanly handled with no placeholder values, duplicate collisions, or lookup errors.
- **Graceful Fallbacks**: Patient tables, exports (CSV, Excel, PDF), dosage reminders, and Follow-Up communications cleanly skip email dispatch without errors when a patient has no email on file.

### 3. Patient App: Recovery Monitoring & Medical History
- **Recovery Monitoring Restored**: Patients can take or upload wound photos, report symptoms (fever, swelling, redness, itching, pain, pus/drainage), add progress notes, and view their recovery timeline.
- **Medical History Table**: Patient app Home tab displays a dedicated Medical History table derived from intake assessment data and vitals.

### 4. Admin Medical Record: Recovery Card & PDF Image Embedding
- **Patient Recovery Card**: Added dedicated "Recovery Notes & Wound Photos" card to the administrative patient record view with photo lightbox zoom and staff moderation controls.
- **Medical Record PDF & Print**: Exported Medical Record PDFs and HTML printouts embed patient recovery photos alongside symptom tags and clinical notes.

### 5. Incident Map Enhancements
- **Wording Cleanup**: Removed all "simulated" / "simulation" terminology across map headers, stat cards, list items, popups, and legends.
- **Official Libon Barangay Labels**: Added 41 official Libon barangay name labels with high-contrast text on satellite imagery, zoom-adaptive density, a "Labels" toggle, and click-to-filter support.
