# K&A Clinic 1.0.21 — 28 October 2026

## Patient App

- **KAI rates**: Asking about prices, including by category or session and in Filipino, shows the clinic's listed rates (PEP Category II and III, Pneumonia vaccine); PrEP and anti-tetanus on their own are referred to the clinic staff.
- **KAI and names**: A name or other personal detail typed in the chat gets a neutral reply; KAI never uses or repeats it and does not thank the user for it.
- **Smarter KAI**: Only short, keyword-style messages (such as "prices" or "clinic hours?") get a fixed answer; full questions (such as "what is the name of this app?") are answered directly.
- **Personal Information**: Height, weight and blood pressure are recorded by the clinic and are read-only in the app.
- **Cancelled appointments**: A patient can delete their own cancelled appointments from the Appointments tab, after a confirmation.
